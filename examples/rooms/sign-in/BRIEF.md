# Sign in

Why does this room exist?

- **For:** someone who has an account and needs to get in, once, on a device they may not own
- **Because:** we want a front door that is boring, quick and impossible to get wrong
- **Matters most:** the person is signed in, or told plainly why not
- **Not doing:** sign-up, password reset, "remember me", social login
